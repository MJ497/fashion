The Atelier

Founded in 2024, Style Atelier Aura is the vision of designer Elizabeth Ushama Ali, who brings a fresh perspective to couture fashion with meticulous attention to detail and sustainable practices.

Each piece is handcrafted in our Parisian-inspired atelier, where traditional techniques meet contemporary design. We celebrate the art of slow fashion, creating garments meant to last and be treasured.
